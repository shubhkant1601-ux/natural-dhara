Seed to Bottle Transparency

How Natural Dhara Crafts Authentic Kachi Ghani

Four disciplined steps ensure nothing is added and nothing natural is stripped away.

01

Mandi Seed Selection

High-oil-content black and brown mustard seeds are sourced directly from trusted North Indian growers.

02

Triple Sieve & Sun Dry

Seeds are thoroughly de-stoned, cleaned, and naturally conditioned without chemical moisture agents.

03

Slow Kolhu Extraction

Pressed gently in low-RPM expellers below 45°C to keep natural aroma, colour, and nutrients intact.

04

Natural Settling & Pack

Gravity-settled and cloth-filtered — never bleached or deodorized — then sealed fresh in Giridih.
