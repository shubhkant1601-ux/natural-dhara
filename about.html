Our Story • Delhi NCR

Born Out of a Simple Promise: Zero Compromise on Purity

Across North India, authentic Kachi Ghani Sarson ka Tel has always been more than a cooking medium — it is the soul of seasonal pickles, crispy parathas, festive sweets, and everyday home-cooked meals. Yet supermarket shelves grew crowded with solvent-extracted, chemically bleached oils masking synthetic pungency.

Founded in Delhi NCR, Natural Dhara set out to restore trust in everyday cooking oil. We source bold, oil-rich black mustard seeds directly from mustard-growing belts of Rajasthan and Haryana, press them slowly below 45°C at our Narela facility, and let the oil settle naturally before sealing it in food-grade bottles, jars, and bulk tins.
