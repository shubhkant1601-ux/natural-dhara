Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 25, 2026 • Natural Dhara Foods (Delhi NCR)

1. Overview

Natural Dhara Foods ("Natural Dhara", "we", "our", or "us"), operating out of Delhi NCR, India, respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal information you share with us when browsing our website, requesting product quotes, or placing orders for our cold-pressed Kachi Ghani mustard oil.

2. Information We Collect

When you submit an enquiry or request a callback through our Contact Us form, we may collect:

  • Your full name and mobile phone number.
  • Email address (if provided voluntarily).
  • Your city or locality within Delhi NCR and delivery notes.
  • Pack quantities and product preferences selected in your Enquiry Bag.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your contact and enquiry details strictly to:

  • Call you back to confirm product availability, pricing, and delivery schedules across Delhi NCR.
  • Provide wholesale quotes for 15L bulk tins or recurring household deliveries.
  • Respond to customer feedback, quality questions, or support requests.

4. Local Storage & Browser Preferences

Our website uses your browser's local storage solely to remember items you add to your Enquiry Bag while navigating between pages. We do not use third-party advertising trackers or sell your browsing behaviour.

5. Data Sharing & Protection

We never sell, rent, or trade your phone number or personal details with marketing agencies. Information may only be shared with our trusted Delhi NCR delivery personnel when needed to complete a requested dispatch, or when required under applicable Indian law.

6. Your Rights & Choices

You may ask us to update or delete any enquiry records associated with your phone number or email at any time by contacting our Delhi NCR support team.

7. Contact Us Regarding Privacy

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please write to us at care@naturaldhara.in or call +91 98110 45290 (Plot 42, Agro Food Park, Narela Industrial Area, New Delhi – 110040).
